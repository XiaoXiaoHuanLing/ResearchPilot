# ResearchPilot Product Overview

## Positioning
ResearchPilot is an AI research platform for open-source topic tracking and analysis. It proactively tracks public information from the internet, helps users build a curated knowledge base, supports retrieval-based Q&A and analysis, and generates topic reports.

## Scope Constraints
- Public, non-classified information only
- Emphasis on source traceability and credibility
- Topic-focused research workflows

## V1 Core Capabilities
1. Topic management
2. Scheduled public information collection
3. Article browsing and filtering
4. Bookmarking into a knowledge base
5. Retrieval-based Q&A with citations
6. Structured report generation

## Typical Use Cases
- Track naval vessel developments
- Track weapons and equipment updates
- Follow public military technology developments
- Build a private research knowledge base
- Generate briefings and topic reports
